# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Administrators and members of the internal Voilier MINIMOCA project team.

## Product Purpose

PROJET OUVERT gives the team one place to coordinate project tasks, meetings, milestones, and budget. Members track their assigned work between meetings; administrators can coordinate work across the team.

## Operating Context

The application supports the team's recurring project planning and execution. Its task board, agenda, milestones, and financial tracking are used together to plan work from meeting to meeting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Tasks are assigned to the current user by default; administrators can assign tasks to other members.
- Dated tasks and scheduled meetings are represented in the agenda.
- The project budget tracks an allocated funding ceiling separately from actual expenditure.
- The application is French-language and supports administrator and member roles.
